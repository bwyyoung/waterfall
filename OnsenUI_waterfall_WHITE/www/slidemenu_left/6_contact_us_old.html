FAQ

1.0 Will I know who the borrowers are?

No. You will be provided with demographic information about borrowers, such as their age, marital status, nationality, location and employment status, but you will not have access to any further sensitive identifying information about them.

2.0 Can I be both an Investor & a borrower?

No.

3.0 How do I withdraw my available credit or investment funds from my account?

You can withdraw credit sitting in your investor account at any time. Go to My Profile > click [Withdraw]. You can withdraw up to the full amount of credit available in your account, however, funds that are currently invested and in funding cannot be withdrawn.
